Operating principles

Free first. Evidence over guesses.

The guide is built around repeatable research, not a single magic solver or a pile of affiliate links.

01 / FREE FIRST

Start with open tools

Every page begins with open-source libraries and practical browser methods before it mentions a paid service.

02 / IDENTIFY FIRST

Read the signals

Network requests, cookies and injected scripts reveal more than the screenshot. Identify the vendor before writing code.

03 / STAY ETHICAL

Respect the target

Use this material for education and authorised testing. Honour terms of service, rate limits and privacy.
